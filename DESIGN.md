# Rebuild decisions

The original prototype was preserved in Git before replacement. The rebuild chose a cohesive stylized direction because browser delivery and freely redistributable assets are practical here; this choice does not itself satisfy a demand for the quality of the best modern games.

The core loop is traverse, read a threat, choose a response, secure a terrace and push upward. The tether serves traversal, crowd control and the guardian's armour mechanic. A spear chain, returning spear, dodge, aerial strike, healing flask and later hunt burst provide tactical choices without an inventory screen interrupting the action.

Eight sectors introduce different combinations of these actions. The waterworks add linked-wheel puzzles, the quarry adds explosive environmental attacks, the turbines add moving hazards, the crown tests charge baiting and exposure windows, and the escape changes the pressure from clearing enemies to reaching safety in time.

The first art inspection exposed missing foliage textures, incorrect facing, intrusive support geometry and poor texture scale. Later inspection added authored ruins, proper material maps, contact shading, a clearer boss camera, foreground fading and recorded effects. Testing also caught a floor snag, a self-refilling burst exploit, input edges lost at high frame rates, and a settings action that could overwrite a saved checkpoint.

The release process is: meaningful simulation checks, full Havok traversal, native browser controls, persistence/replay checks, silent media validation, production build checks, source publication and live-site verification. Screenshots and passing tests do not certify human enjoyment or top-tier visual quality.
